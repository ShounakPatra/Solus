Solus lets you chat with compatible AI models running on your Android device.
Download or import a supported model, then use it for conversation, writing,
coding, or questions about your documents.

Features include chat history, temporary chats, backups, model management,
document previews, and optional tools for compatible models. Image and audio
inputs depend on model and runtime support.

Models are downloaded separately and require storage and memory. Some features,
including model downloads and Wikipedia, use an internet connection. Availability
and performance depend on the selected model, its export, and your device.
